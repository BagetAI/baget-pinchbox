SICHUAN • SPICY & NUMBING

Szechuan Mapo Tofu

Prep Time: 25 Mins
Serves: 2-3

Fresh Shopping List

  • • 1 Pack (14oz) Firm Tofu (cubed)
  • • 4oz Ground Pork (or shiitake minced)
  • • 2 Green Onions (finely sliced)
  • • 1 tbsp Cooking Oil

Pinchbox Sachets

  • Sachet A: Sichuan Peppercorn Blend
  • Sachet B: Authentic Bird's Eye Chili
  • Sachet C: Savory Soy & Sesame Powder
Packaged by IBR Packaging, Carrollton, TX Contains: Sesame, Soy. Gluten-free.

4-Step Weeknight Timeline

Step 1 (Brown & Sauté): Heat oil in skillet. Cook pork/shiitake over high heat until browned and crispy. Add half of the green onions.
Step 2 (Spice Dispersion): Reduce heat to medium. Add Sachet A (Peppercorn) and Sachet B (Chili). Sauté for 45 seconds until intensely aromatic.
Step 3 (Simmer Tofu): Pour in 1/2 cup of water. Stir in Sachet C (Soy-Sesame blend) to build a rich, glossy gravy. Gently slide in cubed tofu. Simmer for 5 minutes.
Step 4 (Finish & Serve): Remove from heat. Garnish with remaining green onions. Serve hot over steamed jasmine rice.
